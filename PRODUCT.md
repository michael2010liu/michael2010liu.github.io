# PRODUCT.md

## What this is
Personal research website for Michael Liu, a student researcher in cognitive neuroscience based in the Bay Area.

## Who visits
- Lab PIs, postdocs and mentors deciding whether to take him on or reply to an email.
- Admissions readers and program reviewers (RSI, BS/MD programs) checking the work behind an application.
- Peers and collaborators looking for a project or a link.

They read on a laptop, usually in daylight at a desk, often arriving from an email signature or an application link. They give the page under a minute.

## What they should leave knowing
1. He works on the brain with EEG, machine learning and simulation, and is learning fMRI.
2. The through-line: the human brain and body under complex or extreme conditions, approached computationally.
3. The concrete projects: Parra Lab EEG work (CCNY), an EEG-based Alzheimer's classifier, an essay on sampling bias in neuroscience, ISS radiation-shielding research, and the tools he has built (MBMNeuro, Signal, Keplr, Monet).
4. How to reach him.

## Constraints
- Every claim must be true. No invented results, metrics, publications or awards.
- Projects in progress are labelled as in progress.
- Static site: plain HTML, CSS and JS. No build step. Hostable on GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.

## Mode
Experience (portfolio): the work leads from the first viewport.
